# Data and code availability — corrected statement (P7-E1, 2026-11-05)

## What the submitted manuscript says

The Data and Code Availability paragraph of the manuscript as
submitted to *Data & Knowledge Engineering* (Ms. No.
DATAK-D-26-02293) states that the replication benchmark, probes,
decision-rule artifact, field-study pre-registration, pipeline and
results, the independent reimplementation, and both upgrade cycles'
pre-registrations, code and results "are preserved with the
manuscript's build record and are intended for public deposit with
the submission package; the corresponding author will provide the
repository location at submission." The portal entry read "Data
will be made available on request."

That deposit was not made at submission, and no repository location
existed. This file corrects the statement.

## Corrected statement

> Data and code availability. The replication benchmark (code and
> results), the bridge-sampling, diagnostic, robustness, and
> control probes reported in the paper, the corrected decision-rule
> artifact with its unit tests, the field study's pre-registration,
> frozen pipeline, and results, both upgrade cycles'
> pre-registrations, code, and results, and the independent
> testers' reimplementation probes are deposited publicly at
> https://github.com/ranadhir19/research/tree/main/paper07_probes
> (deposit completed 2026-11-05 under step P7-E1 of the paper's
> rejection-loop record). The field datasets are public and are not
> redistributed in the deposit; the deposit's
> `field_data/DATA_PROVENANCE.md` identifies each dataset, its
> source and licence, and the SHA-256 of the exact file used, so
> the field runs can be reproduced from the original downloads.
